# Retail-Customer-Retention-Analytics-Target 
## 1. Project Title / Headline

## Retail Customer Retention Analytics Dashboard – Target (Power BI Dashboard)
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn, retention behavior, loyalty effectiveness, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) across regions, channels, and customer segments.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer behavior at Target, helping stakeholders understand why customers churn, who the high-value customers are, and which channels and loyalty strategies drive retention.
It is intended for business analysts, retail strategists, and decision-makers seeking data-driven insights.

## 3. Tech Stack

📊 Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization

📂 Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Measures for churn, CLV, repeat rate, and segmentation

🧩 Data Modeling – Relationships across customer, transaction, store, and loyalty data

📁 File Format – .pbix (development) and .png (dashboard previews)

## 4. Data Source

Simulated Retail Dataset (Target Case Study)

Customer demographics

Transaction and purchase history

Store and channel details (Superstore, Neighborhood, Online)

Loyalty tiers and reward points

Churn indicators and last purchase data

## 5. Features / Highlights
### Business Problem

In a highly competitive retail environment dominated by Walmart, Amazon, and Costco, customer retention is a critical challenge.
Existing reports lack analytical depth to:

Understand churn drivers

Identify loyal vs. at-risk customers

Measure loyalty and promotion effectiveness

Support region- and channel-level strategies

### Goal of the Dashboard

To build an executive-ready Customer Retention Analytics Dashboard that:

Consolidates fragmented customer data

Enables segmentation of Churned, Repeat, and High-Value customers

Measures the impact of loyalty tiers, promotions, and channels

Delivers actionable insights to improve retention and CLV

### Business Impact & Insights

High-value customers generate the most CLV but show higher churn risk

Superstores drive strong revenue but underperform on retention

Promotions increase transaction volume, not average spend

Low reward redemption weakens loyalty engagement

Customer inactivity strongly correlates with churn

##. Key Recommendation

Prioritize retention of high-value and repeat customers, improve high-churn channels, and strengthen loyalty through personalized, redemption-focused engagement.
